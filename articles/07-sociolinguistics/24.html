秘密语言与语言游戏Secret Languages and Language Games

核心概念

秘密语言和语言游戏通过替换、插入、重排或编码规则改变常规表达。它们展示说话者能够有意识地操纵语音和词形规则，也反映群体边界与玩笑性互动。

Core Idea

Secret languages and language games modify ordinary expressions through substitution, insertion, rearrangement, or encoding. They show that speakers can consciously manipulate sound and word-formation patterns and often reinforce group boundaries or playful interaction.
